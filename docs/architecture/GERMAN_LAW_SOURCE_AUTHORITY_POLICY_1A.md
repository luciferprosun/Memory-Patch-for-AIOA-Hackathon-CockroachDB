# German Law source-authority policy 1A

Policy identity: `german-law-source-authority-1a`.

The policy maps the Step 13 legal source class to a Step 9 authority level,
then records authenticity, official publisher, jurisdiction, identifier,
temporal applicability, court identity, verification, consolidation, and
language as separate facts. Missing or conflicting identity, authenticity,
jurisdiction, verification, or temporal facts remain explicit limitations.

- Authentic federal/state promulgation and authentic EU Official Journal acts
  are `OFFICIAL_PRIMARY` for the exact promulgated act.
- Official consolidations are `AUTHORITATIVE_SECONDARY`; they may be useful
  for current wording but are not authentic promulgation.
- An official court decision is primary evidence of that identified decision,
  not universal authority for arbitrary propositions.
- Legislative materials document procedure and history, not enacted law.
- Administrative guidance is agency interpretation, not a replacement for
  legislation or controlling judgments.
- Commentary and private databases remain secondary; user content remains
  user supplied; summaries remain derived.

The output is evidence only. It cannot publish, approve, commit, activate,
perform external actions, or silently resolve same-class conflicts.
