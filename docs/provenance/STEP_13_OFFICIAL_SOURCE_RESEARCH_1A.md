# Step 13 official-source research 1A

Retrieved read-only on 2026-08-01. Only official publishers were used for
policy facts; no corpus was scraped and no paid source was accessed.

- [Gesetze im Internet - Hinweise](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/hinweise.html): the federal electronic promulgation platform publishes the official electronic Bundesgesetzblatt from 1 January 2023; consolidated online statutes are non-official texts and may lag while amendments are incorporated.
- [Bundestag Parlamentsdokumentation](https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/parlamentsdokumentation): DIP provides Bundestag/Bundesrat papers, plenary protocols, and legislative procedure. They are legislative-history evidence, not enacted law.
- [EUR-Lex e-OJ authenticity](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/content/help/oj/authenticity-eOJ.html?locale=en): the electronic Official Journal is the authentic edition under the applicable EU regime. CELEX/consolidated material and summaries must remain distinct from the authentic OJ act.
- [Bundesverfassungsgericht decisions](https://www.bundesverfassungsgericht.de/DE/Entscheidungen/entscheidungen_node.html), [Bundesgerichtshof](https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/DE/Entscheidungen/entscheidungen_node.html), [Bundesarbeitsgericht](https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/entscheidungen/), [Bundesverwaltungsgericht](https://www.bverwg.de/rechtsprechung), [Bundesfinanzhof](https://www.bundesfinanzhof.de/de/entscheidungen/), and [Bundessozialgericht](https://www.bsg.bund.de/DE/Entscheidungen/entscheidungen_node.html): official decision sources. A decision is primary evidence for the identified case; its holding, generalization, hierarchy, and binding effect remain separate legal-analysis questions.

The policy also distinguishes official administrative guidance, official
research/explanatory material, reputable commentary, private databases,
user-supplied documents, derived summaries, and unknown sources. These
categories cannot silently displace conflicting official primary evidence.
